# Access to the full IQ archive

The full RAW/JSON archive is not distributed through this Git repository. Distribution to ESA is being arranged; institutional storage and transfer through UTB/CESNET are under consideration.

The hosting location, authentication procedure, availability period, and download instructions have not yet been finalized. No public download endpoint is currently specified. Publication of this repository does not imply public access to the full IQ archive.

Public derived analysis products are available in [results/](../results/README.md). The [catalog documentation](catalog/README.md) describes the recording inventory, and [example documentation](examples/README.md) describes the separately prepared short excerpts.

Archive paths in the catalogs are relative to the archive root. They are file identifiers, not download URLs. Once an authorized access mechanism is available, preserve the documented station and daily-directory context when downloading recordings; filenames alone do not identify a station.
