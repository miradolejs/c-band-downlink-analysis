# Broadband interference peak counts: 18–30 September 2026

Merged city and village results for detected local maxima in the RF noise-reference band 3836.5–3837.5 MHz. `Peaks_per_5s` counts peaks under the configured threshold and separation rules. The analysis uses eleven five-second chunks beginning at recording offset 0 s.

These are detector counts, rather than counts of independent transmitters or complete interference-event durations. Thresholds and the recorder-referenced dBFS convention are recorded in the MAT metadata.

Processing workflow: [analyze_broadband_impulses_dbfs.m](../../../analysis/broadband_peaks/analyze_broadband_impulses_dbfs.m). The MAT dataset retains analysis settings and separate station results. Figures are provided in PNG for viewing and MATLAB FIG for further inspection. The date interval identifies the result set; it does not independently establish uninterrupted acquisition or successful processing of every scheduled recording.
